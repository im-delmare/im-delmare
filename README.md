# delmare

GEO & AEO strategist helping brands get cited by AI search engines.

I research how AI systems discover, understand and cite brands, with a focus on fintech and embedded finance.

## Work

[delmare.pro](https://delmare.pro) — AI search visibility, GEO and AEO.

## Research

[AI Fintech Visibility Check](https://github.com/im-delmare/ai-fintech-visibility-check) — a practical checklist for testing whether European fintech websites are easy for AI systems to retrieve, understand and cite.

[Embedded Finance Platforms in Europe: Comparison Dataset](https://github.com/im-delmare/embedded-finance-europe-comparison) — a sourced, dated dataset comparing European embedded finance platforms on APIs, banking, payments and lending.

## Case Studies

[Which Embedded Finance Platforms in Europe Offer the Best Infrastructure for Businesses?](https://delmare.pro/2026/11/07/which-embedded-finance-platforms-in-europe-offer-the-best-infrastructure-for-businesses-and-how-do-they-compare-on-apis-banking-payments-and-lending/) — a comparison of European embedded finance providers, their licences, APIs, banking, payments and lending capabilities, supported by a sourced dataset.

[What AI Engines Cite When They Recommend a European Fintech?](https://delmare.pro/2026/10/05/what-ai-engines-cite-when-they-recommend-a-european-fintech/) — a study of which sources AI engines cite when recommending European fintechs, based on 30 responses across ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini.
