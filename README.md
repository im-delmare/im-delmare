# delmare

GEO & AEO strategist researching how brands get discovered, understood and cited by AI search engines.

My work focuses on AI search visibility, with a particular interest in fintech and embedded finance. I combine practical GEO analysis, structured research and observations from real AI-generated answers.

## What I work on

- GEO and AEO strategy for AI search visibility
- How AI systems retrieve, interpret and cite brand information
- Technical and content discoverability for fintech and embedded finance
- Source selection, provider mentions and citation gaps in AI search

## Selected research & case studies

[B2B Fintech Questions in AI Search](https://github.com/im-delmare/b2b-fintech-ai-search-research) — exploratory research documenting 30 responses across 10 B2B fintech questions and ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, including mentioned providers and cited sources. Research snapshot: 9 November 2026.

[Embedded Finance Platforms in Europe: Comparison Dataset](https://github.com/im-delmare/embedded-finance-europe-comparison) — a sourced, dated dataset comparing European embedded finance providers across APIs, banking, payments and lending.

[Which Embedded Finance Platforms in Europe Offer the Best Infrastructure for Businesses?](https://delmare.pro/2026/11/07/which-embedded-finance-platforms-in-europe-offer-the-best-infrastructure-for-businesses-and-how-do-they-compare-on-apis-banking-payments-and-lending/) — a comparison of European providers, their licences, APIs, banking, payments and lending capabilities, supported by a sourced dataset. Published 7 November 2026.

[What Is the Citation Gap in Fintech AI Search?](https://delmare.pro/2026/10/28/what-is-the-citation-gap-in-fintech-ai-search/) — an analysis of the gap between fintech brands being mentioned by AI engines and their own websites being cited as sources. Published 28 October 2026.

[What Do AI Engines Say About Revolut After the September 2026 Data Exposure?](https://delmare.pro/2026/10/13/what-do-ai-engines-say-about-revolut-after-the-september-2026-data-exposure/) — an analysis of six AI responses across three engines, examining factual accuracy, entity clarity and source attribution. Published 13 October 2026.

[What AI Engines Cite When They Recommend a European Fintech?](https://delmare.pro/2026/10/05/what-ai-engines-cite-when-they-recommend-a-european-fintech/) — a study of sources cited when AI engines recommend European fintechs, based on 30 responses across ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini. Published 5 October 2026.

[AI Fintech Visibility Check](https://github.com/im-delmare/ai-fintech-visibility-check) — a practical checklist for assessing whether European fintech websites are easy for AI systems to retrieve, understand and cite.
